# Sounds

- Boss_Laugh and Skeleton_Rattle: cut from "Armored Skeleton" by wowfoxz, Pixabay (Pixabay Content

  crossfaded into itself to loop. License still to check before publishing anything.
- Crowd_Kill_1 and Crowd_Kill_2: cheer.ogg and cheer-crowd.ogg (in tools/audio/source/cheering), from a spell sounds pack, made louder.
  Author and license still to check before publishing anything.
